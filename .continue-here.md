## Decisions

1. **"가벼운" removal (not replacement)**: Rather than trying to guess category-specific keywords, removed the generic keyword entirely. The other keywords in each blog's list already cover relevant products. Safer approach — avoids introducing new irrelevant matches.
2. **SequenceMatcher over prefix LIKE**: Replaced SQL `LIKE '%core%'` with Python `SequenceMatcher` ratio at 80% for title similarity. More accurate, catches semantic similarity not just substring match.
3. **Conservative lookback reduction**: travel2-hugo days=14→7 (not 3), sector-hugo lookback_days=7→3 (more aggressive since sector had 23 failures). Tuned based on data availability per pipeline.
